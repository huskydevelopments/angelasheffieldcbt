Specialisms

Therapies I offer

Every approach below is used flexibly and shaped around you and what you are dealing with, rather than applied as a one-size-fits-all programme.

01

CBT with Exposure and Response Prevention

A specialist form of CBT for obsessive compulsive disorder, helping you face obsessions and break the cycle of compulsions.

02

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy

A practical, structured approach that explores how thoughts, feelings and behaviours connect, and builds skills for change.

03

EMDR

Eye Movement Desensitisation and Reprocessing, a trauma therapy that helps the brain process distressing memories.

04

Trauma-focused CBT

CBT adapted for the effects of trauma, working at a pace that feels manageable to make sense of what happened.

About me

Clinical experience you can rely on

I am a BABCP accredited Cognitive Behavioural Psychotherapist with 14 years of experience, working both in the NHS and in my own small private practice. Before and alongside that, I have spent over 20 years in mental health as a registered mental health nurse.

I provide individually tailored therapy for a wide range of difficulties, including depression, work-related and personal stress, anxiety (worry, panic, social anxiety), obsessive compulsive disorder, trauma and substance misuse.

I am also an accredited EMDR consultant, accredited by EMDR Europe, with 12 years of experience in this therapy.
